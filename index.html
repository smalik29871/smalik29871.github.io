Ghost Note

Page one: Riposte · 128 BPM
  • Cut the crystal Shards as they reach you, along their arrow. Pink for the left Baton, blue for the right.
  • Shoot the brass Horns on the beat, when the ring of light closes.
  • Return the black Rests: cut them on the beat and they fly back at the Eraser that threw them.
  • Play well and the street comes alive: windows, lamps and lights come on. Miss and the band drops out.

On a Quest, open this page in the headset's browser to play in VR. On a screen: move the mouse (or drag a finger) through the Shards and Rests, click or tap to shoot.
